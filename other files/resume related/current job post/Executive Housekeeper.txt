Job Description
Oxford Suites Makati is seeking a dedicated Executive Housekeeper to lead our housekeeping team in delivering pristine accommodations and warm Filipino hospitality. In this key executive role, you will oversee daily room turnovers, public area maintenance, and inventory management across our premier property. Join us in setting the standard for comfort and excellence in Makati's business district.

Responsibilities
Lead and manage daily housekeeping operations, room turnover, and public area cleanliness
Oversee linen handling, chemical safety protocols, and inventory control for guest suites
Prepare staff scheduling, conduct shift supervision, and lead regular staff training programs
Conduct thorough inspections of guest rooms and hotel facilities to ensure top standards
Address guest feedback and execute guest recovery measures for room-related concerns
Ensure full safety compliance and proper maintenance of all housekeeping equipment
Qualifications
Bachelor degree in Hospitality or related field preferred
Proven experience in housekeeping management within a hotel or hospitality setting
Must hold a TESDA Housekeeping NC II certification
First Aid Certificate is an advantage
Strong leadership, problem-solving, and team supervision skills
