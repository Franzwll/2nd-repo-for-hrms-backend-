Job Description
Oxford Suites Makati seeks an experienced General Manager to lead our administrative and operational teams. You will drive hotel performance while delivering our signature warm Filipino hospitality to every guest. Join our premier business district property and elevate our guest experience standards.

Responsibilities
Oversee daily hotel operations across all administrative and service departments
Ensure strict adherence to safety compliance and regulatory standards
Lead staff training initiatives to maintain exceptional service delivery
Manage budget allocation, inventory control, and financial reporting
Handle escalated guest concerns with effective problem solving
Drive revenue growth through strategic upselling and marketing efforts
Qualifications
Proven leadership experience in hotel management or a similar executive role
Degree in hospitality management, business administration, or relevant field
Possession of a valid Food Handler Certificate for safety compliance oversight
Demonstrated expertise in property management systems and hotel operations
