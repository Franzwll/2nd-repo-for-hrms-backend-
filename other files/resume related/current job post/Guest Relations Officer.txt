Job Description
Join the Front Office team at Oxford Suites Makati as a Guest Relations Officer and be the face of our warm Filipino hospitality. In this role, you will deliver exceptional personalized service to our valued corporate and leisure guests in Makati's business district. We are seeking a dedicated hospitality professional who excels in creating memorable guest experiences.

Responsibilities
Welcome guests warmly upon arrival and assist with smooth check-in and check-out procedures
Manage guest feedback, handle inquiries, and execute effective guest recovery when issues arise
Provide personalized assistance and specialized care for VIP Handling throughout their stay
Coordinate closely with Housekeeping and Front Office Operations to ensure prompt room readiness
Promote hotel amenities and maximize revenue through active upselling of room upgrades and services
Maintain complete and accurate guest interaction logs and records documentation in property systems
Qualifications
Bachelor degree in Hospitality or related field, or BS Tourism
TESDA Front Office NC II certification is highly preferred
Demonstrated background in customer service, front desk, or guest relations roles
Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English and Filipino
Amenable to working on a shifting schedule, including weekends and holidays
