Job Description
Oxford Suites Makati is seeking a dedicated HR & Administration Manager to lead our human resources and administrative functions. You will play a vital role in championing our warm Filipino hospitality while maintaining operational excellence. Join our dynamic team in Makati's bustling business district and help shape our workforce culture.

Responsibilities
Manage end-to-end recruitment support and talent acquisition processes
Provide accurate payroll support and maintain meticulous employee records
Coordinate staff training programs aligned with hospitality standards
Ensure strict compliance with labor laws and company policies
Oversee general administrative duties and office inventory control
Handle employee relations, engagement activities, and grievance handling
Qualifications
Proven experience in human resources and general administration management
Possession of a valid First Aid Certificate is an advantage
Strong knowledge of Philippine labor codes and employee relations
Excellent interpersonal skills suitable for a premier hotel environment
