Job Description
Oxford Suites Makati is seeking energetic and customer-focused Bartenders to join our vibrant Food & Beverage team. In this role, you will craft exceptional beverages while delivering warm Filipino hospitality in the heart of Makati's business district. Join us in creating memorable lounge and dining experiences for our guests.

Responsibilities
Prepare and serve high-quality alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages following established recipes
Maintain strict bar hygiene and clean work areas according to sanitation standards
Operate POS systems accurately to process guest orders and manage cash handling
Engage with guests warmly, provide beverage recommendations, and drive upselling opportunities
Monitor bar stock levels, perform routine mise en place, and assist in inventory control
Ensure responsible alcohol service and address guest inquiries with excellent communication
Qualifications
Must hold a TESDA Bartending NC II or Food Handler Certificate
Demonstrated knowledge in mixology, beverage preparation, and bar service
Strong communication and customer service skills in a hospitality setting
Willingness to work a flexible schedule, including night shifts, weekends, and holidays
Familiarity with POS systems and basic inventory procedures is preferred
