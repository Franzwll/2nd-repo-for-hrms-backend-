Job Description
Join Oxford Suites Makati as a Floor Supervisor to lead our housekeeping team in delivering exceptional guest experiences. You will oversee daily room turnover and ensure our high standards of cleanliness and warmth are consistently met. Be part of a premier all-suite hotel where Filipino hospitality shines brightest every single day.

Responsibilities
Supervise daily housekeeping operations and room turnover across assigned floors
Inspect guest rooms and public areas to ensure strict adherence to quality standards
Conduct staff training and mentor housekeeping attendants for optimal performance
Manage linen handling and maintain accurate inventory levels of cleaning supplies
Report maintenance issues promptly and coordinate with engineering for timely repairs
Handle guest requests and resolve complaints with genuine Filipino hospitality
Qualifications
Holder of TESDA Housekeeping NC II certification
Proven supervisory or leadership experience in hotel housekeeping operations
Knowledgeable in chemical safety and proper housekeeping procedures
Flexible and willing to work on a shifting schedule for full-time employment
Possesses strong interpersonal and leadership abilities
