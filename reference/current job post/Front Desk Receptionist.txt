Job Description
Join Oxford Suites Makati as a Front Desk Receptionist and become the face of our renowned warmth and Filipino hospitality in the heart of Makati's business district. In this dynamic role, you will deliver seamless guest experiences from arrival to departure while upholding our commitment to premier service standards. We are looking for dedicated professionals ready to excel in a full-time, shifting schedule setup.

Responsibilities
Perform efficient guest check-in and check-out operations following hotel standards
Handle guest inquiries, reservations, and requests promptly with warm Filipino hospitality
Manage cash handling, bill settlements, and accurate posting of charges using POS systems
Resolve guest complaints and coordinate guest recovery efforts effectively
Maintain accurate guest records documentation and proper turn-over during shift changes
Promote hotel amenities and maximize revenue through proactive upselling techniques
Qualifications
Bachelor degree in Hospitality or related field, or BS Tourism is preferred
TESDA Front Office NC II certification is a strong advantage
Prior experience in Front Office Operations or hotel guest services is preferred
Demonstrated proficiency in Property Management Systems and MS Office
Willingness to work on a shifting schedule, including weekends and holidays
